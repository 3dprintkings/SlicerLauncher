They should not be used in a way that is likely to imply that an unofficial fork, modified build, redistribution, or commercial offering is produced, sponsored, approved, or endorsed by 3DPrintKings when it is not.

## Third-party names

SlicerLauncher refers to third-party products such as Bambu Studio, ELEGOO Slicer, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer, Creality Print, and Autodesk Fusion 360 solely to describe compatibility and workflow integration.

Those product names and any associated trademarks belong to their respective owners. Their mention does not imply affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement unless explicitly stated otherwise.

No third-party slicer software is bundled with SlicerLauncher.
